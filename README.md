# 👥 HR Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

## Overview

This project uses **Power BI to analyze employee and workforce data** and develop an interactive HR analytics dashboard.

The dashboard provides a comprehensive view of workforce composition, employee attrition, demographics, job satisfaction, education, and departmental trends. The goal was to transform employee-level data into actionable insights that could support workforce planning and HR decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The objective of this project was to analyze employee data and answer key HR-related questions, including:

* How large is the organization's workforce?
* What percentage of employees have left the organization?
* Which departments experience the highest levels of attrition?
* How does attrition vary across age groups and gender?
* How is the workforce distributed across different job roles?
* What patterns exist in employee job satisfaction?
* How does attrition vary by education field?
* What demographic characteristics are associated with employee turnover?

---

## 🛠️ Tools

* **Power BI** — Dashboard development and interactive data visualization
* **Power Query** — Data preparation and transformation
* **DAX** — Measures, KPIs, and calculated metrics
* **Excel / CSV** — Source employee data

---

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators

The dashboard provides an overview of the organization's workforce:

| KPI                         |  Value |
| --------------------------- | -----: |
| Total Employees             |  1,470 |
| Active Employees            |  1,233 |
| Employees Lost to Attrition |    237 |
| Attrition Rate              | 16.12% |
| Average Employee Age        |     37 |

These KPIs provide a high-level snapshot of the organization's workforce and employee retention.

---

## 🏢 Attrition by Department

Employee attrition was analyzed across:

* Research & Development
* Sales
* Human Resources

The dashboard allows users to compare attrition across departments and identify areas where employee turnover may warrant further investigation.

---

## 👤 Employee Demographics

The dashboard examines the workforce across several demographic dimensions, including:

* Age
* Gender
* Education
* Education field

Employees are grouped into age ranges including:

* Under 25
* 25–34
* 35–44
* 45–54
* Over 55

This allows demographic patterns in the workforce and employee attrition to be explored interactively.

---

## 💼 Job Role Analysis

Employee distribution and job satisfaction were analyzed across multiple job roles, including:

* Healthcare Representative
* Human Resources
* Laboratory Technician
* Manager
* Manufacturing Director
* Research Director
* Research Scientist
* Sales Executive
* Sales Representative

The dashboard incorporates a **Job Satisfaction Survey** ranging from 1 to 4, allowing satisfaction levels to be compared across different roles.

---

## 🎓 Education & Attrition

The project also examines attrition across different education fields, including:

* Life Sciences
* Medical
* Marketing
* Technical Degree
* Other
* Human Resources

This analysis helps identify whether employee turnover is concentrated within particular educational backgrounds.

---

## 📈 Attrition by Age & Gender

Employee attrition was further segmented by both age and gender.

The dashboard provides comparisons across age groups to identify differences in workforce turnover patterns.

This type of segmentation can help HR teams investigate whether retention challenges are concentrated among specific employee populations.

---

## 💡 Business Applications

The analysis demonstrates how HR data can be used to support:

* Employee retention analysis
* Workforce planning
* Department-level staffing decisions
* Employee satisfaction analysis
* Demographic analysis
* Organizational workforce monitoring
* Identification of potential turnover patterns

The dashboard is designed to allow stakeholders to move from high-level workforce KPIs into more detailed demographic and employee analyses.
